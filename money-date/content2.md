WEBSITE PROPOSAL
Money Date with Esther Odejobi 2.0
Premium Event Website Proposal
Prepared For: Esther Odejobi
Prepared By: Insight Nova Tech Ltd
Prepared By: Sylver Oyinaga
Date: July 2026

Executive Summary
Thank you for the opportunity to propose the design and development of the official website for Money Date with Esther Odejobi 2.0.
This proposal outlines a modern, premium event website that will serve as the digital home for the event—communicating its vision, attracting attendees, showcasing speakers and partners, and driving registrations.
Rather than creating a simple registration page, the objective is to build a high-quality digital experience that reflects the prestige and purpose of the Money Date brand while positioning it as one of Nigeria’s leading financial wellness experiences.

Project Objectives
The website will aim to:
Increase event awareness
Drive ticket registrations
Communicate the event vision clearly
Showcase speakers and partners professionally
Build credibility and trust
Provide attendees with all event information in one place
Create excitement before, during, and after the event

Design Direction
The website will feature a clean, elegant and premium design inspired by leading international conferences while maintaining a unique identity for Money Date.
The design language will communicate:
Excellence
Luxury
Simplicity
Professionalism
Modern financial lifestyle
Wellness
Community
The overall experience will emphasize storytelling rather than simply displaying information.
